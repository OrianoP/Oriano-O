# Photos

- `menu/` — one photo per dish, named after the dish in lowercase with dashes:
  `classic-margherita.webp`, `pepperoni-overload-ranch.webp`, `pepsi-original.webp` …
- `hero.webp` — the big photo at the top of the home page (wide, landscape).
- `story.webp` — the photo next to the "New York style" section (portrait or square).

Use `.webp` or `.jpg`, about 1600px on the longest side. They're optimised automatically by Next.js.
